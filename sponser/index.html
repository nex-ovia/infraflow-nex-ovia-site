Why sponsor?

Support the Nexovia Standard

Open standards only work when they are maintained, communicated, and built on. The Nexovia Standard is free to use, free to adopt, and free to build on. Sponsorship funds the time to develop the tooling, maintain the standard, publish new versions, and grow the community around it.

Tooling development

Building the NXS CLI, Visualiser, and Docs tools that make the standard actionable.

Standard maintenance

Researching, writing, and publishing new versions of NXS as delivery practices evolve.

Community and content

Writing, speaking, and publishing to grow awareness and adoption of delivery sovereignty.

Infrastructure

Hosting, domains, and tooling to keep nex-ovia.com and the GitHub org running.
