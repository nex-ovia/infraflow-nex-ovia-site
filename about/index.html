Why I wrote NXS

I kept watching the same failure. Something gets built, the person who built it moves on, and nobody left can explain it, change it or move it.

AI makes that worse. When code is cheap to write, understanding becomes the scarce thing. NXS is four rules that keep any solution understood and owned, whoever or whatever built it.

My foundation is hands-on: Linux, open-source infrastructure, real systems. So the standard is built on how systems actually behave, not on how they look on a slide.
