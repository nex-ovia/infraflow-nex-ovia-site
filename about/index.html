Why I wrote NXS

Most enterprise implementations fail the same way. Something gets built, the person who built it moves on, and nobody left can explain, maintain or extend what was delivered.

AI makes this worse, not better. When code is cheap to generate, the scarce thing is understanding. NXS is my answer: four rules that keep any solution owned, understood and portable, whoever or whatever built it.

I use it on everything I ship, including this website.
