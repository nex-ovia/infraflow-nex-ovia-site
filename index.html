The Four Rules

Not guidelines.
Not suggestions.

Every NXS solution follows four rules. Each one exists because it directly protects delivery sovereignty — the principle that what you build belongs to you, not to the tool that built it.

RULE 01

Logic separate from tools

The business rules live somewhere identifiable and independent. If the vendor changes, the logic does not.

RULE 02

Configuration externalised

Nothing environment-specific lives inside the solution. It runs identically everywhere — local, staging, production.

RULE 03

Dependencies visible

Every external connection is named and documented. No hidden surface area. No surprises during a handover.

RULE 04

Runs anywhere, for anyone

No tribal knowledge required. No original builder needed. Anyone can run it from what is written down.

Delivery Sovereignty

Delivery sovereignty means the team that commissions a solution retains full understanding, full control, and full portability of what was built — regardless of what tool built it, what AI generated it, or who originally delivered it.
